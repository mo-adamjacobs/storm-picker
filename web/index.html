Persistence map

Click to select time and distance thresholds for clustering. The inner region (not always visible) is where the exact selected cluster exists, outer region is where a cluster within the given tolerance (right side of screen) exists, and dotted line is where all strikes join into a single cluster.

"Stability" is the proportion of the outer region's area relative to the whole box.
